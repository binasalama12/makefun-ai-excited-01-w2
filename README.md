# ai-excited-01 — w2: Rainbow Fountain

Golden hour in an ordinary backyard, and then — lift-off. A little girl launches herself straight through the sprinkler's arc, arms thrown wide, hair flying, laughing mid-air while the spray catches the low sun and throws a small rainbow across the frame. No setup, no posing: just one frozen second of a summer afternoon turning into magic.

## Why it excites

- **Excitement:** the leap is at its peak — body fully airborne, energy at maximum, the whole frame feels like it is still moving.
- **Surprise:** a rainbow where nobody expects one, drawn out of garden water and evening light.
- **Momentum:** the sprinkler's arc, the spray, and her trajectory all pull the eye in the same upward direction.
- **Craft:** warm golden-hour grading, crisp droplets, and a clean 1024×1024 frame that holds up at full size.
- **Reads small:** the silhouette, the arc of water, and the rainbow band are all legible at a glance, even as a tiny thumbnail.

## Run

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) and logs its byte size.
