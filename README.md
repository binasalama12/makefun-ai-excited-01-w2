## Rainbow Fountain

Golden hour on an ordinary lawn. A little girl takes a running leap straight
through the sprinkler spray — arms wide, hair flying, mid-air and laughing —
and the low sun catches the mist so a rainbow blooms right through the water
around her. No filter, no setup: just one summer-second, frozen at the top
of the jump, when the backyard turns into a fountain of light.

## Why it excites

- **Excitement** — a child airborne at the peak of the jump, all motion and
  joy, no posed stillness anywhere in the frame.
- **Surprise** — the rainbow is a gift of the light, not a prop: it appears
  inside the spray exactly where the leap happens.
- **Momentum** — the arc of the jump, the spray fanning out, the sun low and
  warm: the whole frame leans forward into the next second.
- **Craft** — golden-hour grading, crisp spray detail against a soft lawn,
  and the rainbow held sharp without washing out the subject.
- **Reads small** — one bold silhouette, one bright arc, one warm glow: the
  image still lands at thumbnail size.
