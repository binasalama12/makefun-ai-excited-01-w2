## The moment

Golden hour in the garden. A little girl — about seven, red raincoat, yellow
boots — has just discovered that the sprinkler is not a chore to dodge but a
fountain to jump through. She is caught mid-leap, arms thrown wide, laughing
with her whole face, while the low sun catches the spray and throws a clear
rainbow straight across the mist. Water droplets hang frozen in the air
around her, her hair flies, and for one frame the whole garden is a
celebration. It is the exact second a normal summer evening turns into the
best day of the summer.

## Why it excites

- **Excitement / jolt** — a child mid-air, mouth open in a full laugh, red
  coat against green grass: the joy hits before you even parse the scene.
- **Surprise** — a rainbow appears out of a garden sprinkler, an ordinary
  object doing something magical, low sun flare and all.
- **Momentum** — frozen leap, flying hair, droplets suspended mid-air: the
  whole frame is moving, and you feel the landing coming.
- **Craft** — photorealistic golden-hour light, dense spray detail, low
  camera angle, and a rainbow that reads clearly at any size.
- **Reads small** — one big red figure, one bright rainbow arc, one sun
  flare: the image is legible at thumbnail size in a single glance.
